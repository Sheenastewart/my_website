My Professional Journey

My career has taken me through several industries, but technology, problem-solving, and a desire to understand how things work have always been at the center of it.

I began my professional journey in telecommunications and technical support at DirecTV, where I developed a strong foundation in troubleshooting, customer service, and technical problem-solving. I later transitioned into hands-on technical roles at Comcast and ADT, gaining extensive experience installing, wiring, maintaining, and repairing telecommunications equipment, networking systems, and low-voltage electrical and electronic systems.

During my time at ADT, I particularly enjoyed working directly with electrical circuits, relays, control boards, access control systems, surveillance equipment, and other electronic technologies. There was something rewarding about diagnosing a problem, figuring out why something wasn't working, and being able to fix it myself. Those experiences strengthened my technical abilities and deepened my interest in how technology works behind the scenes.

As I began planning my next chapter, I knew I wanted to pursue a college degree in Software Engineering. I intentionally transitioned from field service into banking at PNC, seeking a less physically demanding role that would allow me to pursue my education while continuing my professional career.

As I progressed through my degree program, I recognized an opportunity to accelerate my education by dedicating myself to it full-time. I ultimately made the decision to step away from banking and focus entirely on completing my degree, strengthening my programming skills, and preparing for my transition into software engineering.

In August 2026, I accomplished that goal and graduated from Western Governors University with my Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering. Throughout my education, I gained experience with Java, Python, JavaScript, SQL, Spring Boot, REST APIs, databases, software testing, and application development.

Today, I'm excited to bring together both sides of my technical background: years of hands-on experience working with electrical, electronic, and communication systems, along with my education in software engineering.

I'm currently exploring opportunities in software development, engineering, systems integration, application support, software testing, and technical operations. I'm particularly interested in roles where I can continue learning, solve meaningful technical challenges, and contribute to innovative projects.

My career has taught me that growth doesn't always mean starting over. Sometimes, it means making intentional decisions, investing in yourself, and finding new ways to apply everything you've learned. That's exactly what I'm doing as I move into this next chapter.
